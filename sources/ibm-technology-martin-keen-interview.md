# IBM Technology — Martin Keen Interview

Interview by Justin Brady with Martin Keen, host of IBM Technology YouTube channel (1M+ subscribers).

## Key Points

### Audience-First, Not Brand-First
- **"The policy is don't talk about the IBM offering."** IBM Technology educates agnostically — no product promotion.
- "People don't care about your brand at all. They care about themselves. They care about entertaining, interesting content."

### The Indirect Business Case
- "When you're thinking about an AI topic like large language models, if you've watched five or six IBM videos about them, even if they didn't mention the IBM offering, IBM is now part of that conversation."
- Direct tracking exists (CTAs, description links, product page referrals) but the real value is unmeasured brand association.

### Growth Formula
1. **Start broad** — "101" explainer videos attract large audiences via search.
2. **Drill down** — lead audiences into deeper, more technical content over time.
3. **SEO-driven topic selection** — find high-volume underserved search terms *before* producing. His breakthrough beer channel video took off because it addressed a popular yet underserved search term.

### "Versus" Content
- "Comparing one thing to another is absolute catnip for YouTube." Keen uses comparisons on both IBM Technology and his personal brewing channel (Brewlosophy).

### Personal Channel
- Keen also runs Brewlosophy (tens of thousands of subscribers), applying the same principles — demonstrating that audience-first content strategy is transferable across domains.

## Relevance to Production Guidelines
- Core validation of the "viewer-first" principle: educate genuinely, brand benefits follow naturally.
- SEO-driven topic selection as a pre-production step.
- "Versus" content as a reliable high-performing format.
