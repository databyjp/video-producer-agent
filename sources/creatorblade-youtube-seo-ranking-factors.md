# CreatorBlade — YouTube SEO 2026: 18 Ranking Factors

CreatorBlade's analysis of 6,300 high-ranking videos across 24 niches (late 2025–early 2026). **Evidence class: external hypothesis.** It is a correlational vendor study, not a published YouTube ranking formula. Its tiers, thresholds, percentages, and claims about "new" signals should be treated as hypotheses to test against channel data.

## Useful hypotheses

- Packaging, viewer retention, and a clear match to search intent are likely more consequential than tag optimization.
- Specific long-tail queries can be useful topic prompts for developer content.
- Related videos, end screens, and playlists can help viewers find an appropriate next video.
- Accurate captions and unique video-specific descriptions improve usability and clarity.

## Claims not to promote as platform facts

The original article gives precise values for first-hour CTR, retention by duration, returning-viewer windows, topic authority, caption indexing lift, end-screen CTR, comment/like ratios, schedule impact, and a mandatory chapter length. YouTube does not document these as universal thresholds or ranking factors.

Use the official [YouTube Help summary](youtube-search-discovery-official.md) for platform behavior. Use this source only when explicitly attributing an external hypothesis.

### 30-minute pre-publish stack

5 min thumbnail testing → 5 min title optimization → 5 min keyword selection (primary + 3 long-tail) → 5 min description → 5 min end screens/cards → 5 min upload SRT.

## Relevance to DevRel

- Focused topic clusters and specific technical queries are sensible DevRel experiments, not algorithm guarantees.
- Correct technical captions are valuable for accessibility and comprehension; measure any discovery impact in channel analytics.
