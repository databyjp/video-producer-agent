Will vector search improve your product?

Here's what it does, and when you need it.

Traditional search uses exact matching, which works really well for product IDs, or specific terms. But for related ideas or long descriptions, not so much. 

Vector search solves this by capturing the meaning of inputs, and putting them into "vector space" where similar things are closer to each other.

It can find similar words, paragraphs, or even images. It can even work across different languages or content types.

So, if your search needs include the word "like" something, vector search is what you want. 

Or use hybrid search, which combines results from traditional and vector searches. 

Choose the right search based on your needs.
