# YouTube SEO Fundamentals

Understanding discovery is useful context for packaging and metadata decisions. The core insight: **YouTube matches videos to viewers, not keywords to a generic ranking formula.** Search considers relevance, engagement, and quality; recommendations also use viewer personalization and satisfaction. ([official source](../sources/youtube-search-discovery-official.md))

## The Mental Model

Use this as a working model, not YouTube terminology:

1. **Be understandable.** Title, description, and video content should make the topic and intended viewer clear.
2. **Be chosen and satisfying.** A viewer must choose the video, receive the promised value, and feel their time was well spent.

Tags are low leverage; a clear promise and satisfying content are not.

## What YouTube Documents

| Area | Practical implication |
|---|---|
| Appeal | A title and thumbnail need a clear, accurate reason to choose the video. |
| Engagement | Review both average view duration and average percentage viewed; interpret them relative to format and viewer intent. |
| Satisfaction | Viewer feedback, likes, and survey signals matter alongside watch behavior. |
| Search relevance | Make the title, description, and video itself clearly match the problem being solved. |
| Quality | Demonstrate expertise and trustworthy technical guidance. |

YouTube publishes no universal CTR, retention, comment-rate, title-position, publishing-cadence, or returning-viewer thresholds. Use your own channel baseline when setting targets.

## What This Means for Developer Content

1. **Build coherent viewer expectations.** A focused set of related topics can help people understand why they should return; it is a channel strategy, not a published threshold.
2. **Use specific problems as topic prompts.** “Deploy Hono to Cloudflare Workers with D1” expresses a clearer job than “Cloudflare tutorial.” Validate demand and outcomes in Studio and Google Trends.
3. **Review technical captions.** Accuracy improves accessibility and prevents a confusing experience for viewers relying on captions.
4. **Treat packaging and retention as connected.** Accurate packaging earns the right click; the video must immediately deliver that promise.
5. **Use tools for research and experiments, not as a substitute for the video.** Native A/B testing can improve packaging; no tool can repair an unclear or unhelpful video.
