## Objective

A graphic showing a video being decomposed into its three information layers. Used to explain what can be embedded and why the choice matters.

## Content

A single video being separated into three distinct layers:

1. **Visual** — what's on screen (images, graphics, people).
2. **Speech** — the spoken words / transcript.
3. **Audio** — non-speech sound (music, sound effects, ambient noise).

The key point: speech and audio are separate layers. Audio carries meaningful information beyond speech (e.g. a rocket launch has lots of audio but no speech).
