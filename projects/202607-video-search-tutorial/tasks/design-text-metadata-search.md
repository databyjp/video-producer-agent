## Objective

A simple graphic showing that conventional video search relies on text metadata, not the actual video content. Used as an explainer overlay early in the video.

## Content

Show a search bar with a query being used to search videos. The key idea: the search matches against **text metadata** (title, description, transcript) — not the visual content of the video itself.

The visual layer of the video should be visibly ignored or bypassed. The text metadata should be clearly what the search is connecting to.

Labels to include: "Title", "Description", "Transcript" (as the text metadata being searched).
