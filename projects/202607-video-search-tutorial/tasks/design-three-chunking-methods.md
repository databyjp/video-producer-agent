## Objective

A graphic showing the three video chunking strategies at a glance. Used as a quick visual summary before each method is explained in detail.

## Content

Three methods, each with a short label and a simple representation of how it splits a video:

1. **Fixed length** — video split into equal-duration segments.
2. **Transcript-based (semantic)** — video split at natural topic/sentence boundaries in the spoken content.
3. **Visual (scene-based)** — video split where the visuals change (e.g. shot changes, graphics changes).

Each method should be clearly distinguishable from the others. The emphasis is on *where* the cuts happen and *why* they differ.
