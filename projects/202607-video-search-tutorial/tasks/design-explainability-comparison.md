## Objective

A small comparison graphic highlighting the difference in explainability between keyword/BM25 search and vector/embedding search. Used during the tradeoffs discussion near the end of the video.

## Content

Two sides:

**Keyword / BM25 search:**
- Based on exact term matches and a formula.
- You can verify and explain *why* a result was returned.

**Vector / embedding search:**
- Based on deep learning models.
- Results are useful but hard to explain — "black box."
- You can make educated guesses but can't break down exactly why a result ranked where it did.

Keep it compact — this is a supporting point, not a centerpiece.
