Two useful boundaries

Keep in mind
Raw vs. summary

raw/ keeps originals; sources/ and wiki/ contain maintained summaries and reusable synthesis.

Project vs. wiki

Keep video-specific decisions and deliverables in projects/<video>/. Promote reusable lessons to wiki/.
